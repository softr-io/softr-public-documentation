A workspace is where your Softr projects live (apps, databases, workflows, forms and agents) together with the people who build them. Each workspace has its own plan and billing, its own members, and can have teams that keep some projects private to a group.

When you log in to Softr Studio, you land on **Home** in your current workspace. The workspace switcher at the top of the left sidebar shows which workspace you're in and which plan it's on.

<Frame caption="Softr Studio Home. The workspace switcher sits at the top of the left sidebar.">
  ![Softr Studio Home screen with an arrow pointing at the workspace switcher in the top-left corner, which shows the workspace name and its plan](/images/workspaces-and-applications/studio-home.png)
</Frame>

Every project in the workspace is listed under **All projects** in the left sidebar, with tabs for **Apps**, **Databases**, **Workflows**, **Forms**, **Agents** and **Integrations**.

## Create a new workspace

You get one workspace when you sign up. You can create more, for example one per client or one for a side project on its own plan, and other people can invite you into their workspaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Switch workspace">
    Click the workspace switcher at the top of the left sidebar, hover **Switch workspace**, and click **Add**.

    <Frame caption="Add sits at the bottom of the Switch workspace list.">
      ![Workspace menu open with the Switch workspace submenu showing the current workspace with a check mark and an arrow pointing at Add](/images/workspaces-and-applications/add-workspace.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the workspace">
    Enter a **Workspace Name**, choose a **Workspace icon** if you like, and click **Create workspace**.

    <Frame caption="The New Workspace dialog.">
      ![New Workspace dialog with a Workspace Name field, a Workspace icon swatch, and Cancel and Create workspace buttons](/images/workspaces-and-applications/new-workspace-dialog.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite people">
    Add the people you'll build with. See [Invite members](#invite-members).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To move between workspaces, open the switcher, hover **Switch workspace**, and pick one. A check mark shows the workspace you're in.

## The workspace menu

Clicking the workspace switcher opens the workspace menu. The top section manages the workspace you're in; the section below it is for your own account. Show below are options for the current workspace (1) and where to Switch workspaces (2).

<Frame caption="The workspace menu: options for the current workspace (1) and Switch workspace (2).">
  ![Workspace menu with a box around Settings, Plan & billing, Members and Audit logs marked 1, and an arrow marked 2 pointing at Switch workspace](/images/workspaces-and-applications/workspace-menu.png)
</Frame>

- **Settings**: the workspace name, icon and ID.
- **Plan & billing**: the workspace's plan, usage, AI credits and payment details.
- **Members**: who is in the workspace and their role.
- **Audit logs**: a record of activity in the workspace.
- **Switch workspace**: move to another workspace or create a new one.
- **My account**, **API tokens** and **Use Softr from** apply to your own account in every workspace. See [Connect your AI assistant](/mcp/connect) for **Use Softr from**.

## Workspace settings

**Settings** opens **Workspace info**. Here the workspace owner can:

1. Rename the workspace: edit **Workspace name** and click **Save**.
2. Copy the **Workspace ID**, which support and the Softr API may ask for.

You can also change the **Workspace icon** here.

<Frame caption="Workspace info: the workspace name (1) and the Workspace ID copy button (2).">
  ![Workspace info page with an arrow marked 1 pointing at the Workspace name field and its Save button, and an arrow marked 2 pointing at the Workspace ID copy button](/images/workspaces-and-applications/workspace-settings.png)
</Frame>

## Invite members

**Members** lists everyone in the workspace with their role. The workspace owner is marked **Workspace owner**.

<Frame caption="The Members page. Invite is in the top-right corner.">
  ![Members page listing the workspace owner with the Owner role, and an arrow pointing at the Invite button](/images/workspaces-and-applications/workspace-members.png)
</Frame>

To add someone:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    On the **Members** page, click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Type the person's email address and select **Add** in the suggestion that appears. Repeat for anyone else you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Invite** to send the invitations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Invite members to the workspace by email.">
  ![Invite members dialog with an email address typed in the field, an Invite button, and the workspace owner listed below](/images/workspaces-and-applications/invite-members.png)
</Frame>

Members see every project that isn't inside a team. To give someone access to one project only, share that project instead. See [Share a single project](#share-a-single-project).

## Share a single project

You can share one app, database or workflow without giving someone the rest of the workspace. This is how you bring in a freelancer, an agency or a client to work on a single project.

Open the sharing dialog in either of two ways:

- On **All projects**, click the **⋯** menu on the project card and choose **Share**.
- Inside the project in Studio, click the **Share** icon in the top bar, next to Preview and **Publish**.

<Frame caption="Share in a project's menu on All projects.">
  ![Project card menu open with Duplicate, Share, Move to, Create a template, View connections, Change thumbnail and Delete, and an arrow pointing at Share](/images/workspaces-and-applications/project-menu-share.png)
</Frame>

<Frame caption="The Share icon in the Studio top bar.">
  ![Softr Studio top bar with an arrow pointing at the Share icon to the left of the Preview and Publish buttons](/images/workspaces-and-applications/share-from-studio.png)
</Frame>

In the dialog, type a name or email, select **Add**, pick a role, and click **Invite**.

<Frame caption="Share a project by email and choose the role they get.">
  ![Share Untitled Application dialog with an email address and the Editor role selected in the invite field, the Invite button, and the project owner listed below](/images/workspaces-and-applications/share-project.png)
</Frame>

The dialog lists everyone who has access and their role. When you share an app, the people you share it with also get indirect access to the databases and integrations the app is connected to, so they can keep building. The **connected items** link at the bottom of the dialog shows which ones.

## Teams

A team is a private space inside the workspace, with its own projects and its own members. Projects in a team are visible only to the members of that team and the workspace owner. Anything not in a team stays visible to every workspace member under **All projects**.

To create a team, go to **All projects**, click **Add team**, enter a name and click **Next**.

<Frame caption="Create a team from All projects.">
  ![Add new team dialog with a Name field, an icon picker, and Cancel and Next buttons](/images/workspaces-and-applications/add-team.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  How many teams you can create depends on your workspace's plan. See [Pricing and Plans](/workspace-and-billing/pricing-and-plans).
</Note>

## Roles and permissions

Access in Softr works at three levels: the workspace, teams, and individual projects.

### Workspace roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | One per workspace. Full control of settings, plan and billing, members, teams and every project. The owner can [transfer ownership](/workspace-and-billing/transfer-workspace-ownership) to another member. |
| **Member** | Sees and builds every project that isn't in a team, plus the teams they're added to and anything shared with them directly. |
| **Guest** | Sees nothing by default. A guest only has access to the teams or projects shared with them, which suits freelancers, agencies and clients. |

### Project roles

These are the roles you choose in a project's **Share** dialog.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Can share, edit and delete the project. Each project has one owner, and ownership can be handed over with **Change owner**. The workspace owner has the same rights on every project. |
| **Editor** | Can edit the project, but can't manage who has access. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access. When you share an app, Viewer is listed as coming soon. |

<Tip>
  A person's access is the highest role they hold at any level. Adding someone to a team or sharing a project with them can widen their access, but never narrows access they already have.
</Tip>

## Plan and billing

Each workspace is billed on its own, with its own plan. Everyone who works in the workspace uses the features that plan includes.

**Plan & billing** shows:

- the current plan, and the options to change or downgrade it,
- usage against your plan's limits: app users, published apps, custom domains, database records, file storage and workflow actions,
- your AI credits, with the option to buy more,
- your payment method, billing details and invoices.

<Frame caption="Plan & billing shows the plan, usage and AI credits for this workspace.">
  ![Plan & billing page with the current plan on the left, usage cards for Apps, Databases and Workflows in the middle, and AI credits on the right](/images/workspaces-and-applications/plan-and-billing.png)
</Frame>

Only the workspace owner can manage the plan and billing. For details, see [Billing](/workspace-and-billing/billing), [Pricing and Plans](/workspace-and-billing/pricing-and-plans) and [AI Credits](/workspace-and-billing/ai-credits).

## Move a project

You can move a project to another workspace, or into a team within the same workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Move to">
    On **All projects**, click the **⋯** menu on the project card and choose **Move to**.

    <Frame caption="Move to in a project's menu.">
      ![Project card menu open with an arrow pointing at Move to](/images/workspaces-and-applications/project-menu-move.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination">
    Pick the destination workspace from the dropdown, then choose **All projects** or a team in that workspace, and click **Move**.

    <Frame caption="The Move dialog. Here the workspace has no teams yet, so there is nowhere else to move the app.">
      ![Move Untitled Application to dialog with a workspace dropdown, All projects marked Current, and Cancel and Move buttons](/images/workspaces-and-applications/move-project.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The destination workspace's plan needs to support the features the project uses, such as its number of app users or single sign-on. If the project uses data sources connected in the current workspace, you may need to reconnect them after the move.
</Note>

## Duplicate or delete a project

Both options are in the project's **⋯** menu on **All projects**.

<Frame caption="Duplicate (1) and Delete (2) in a project's menu.">
  ![Project card menu with an arrow marked 1 pointing at Duplicate and an arrow marked 2 pointing at Delete](/images/workspaces-and-applications/project-menu-duplicate-delete.png)
</Frame>

- **Duplicate** makes a copy of the project in its current state, in the same workspace.
- **Delete** removes the project. Confirm in the dialog that follows. Only the project's owner, or the workspace owner, can delete it.

<Warning>
  Deleting a project removes it for everyone who has access to it. If you might need a copy, duplicate it first.
</Warning>
