<Note>Kanban block is available for users on the [Professional plan and above](https://www.softr.io/pricing).</Note>

<Frame caption="A Kanban board of tasks in a Softr app.">![A Kanban board with four columns: To do, In progress, In review, and Done](/building-blocks/images/kanban/board.webp)</Frame>

The Kanban block shows records from a [data source](/4230abecf813402aa2926466c313290a) as cards in columns, like a [Kanban board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanban_board). Each column is one value of a field, such as a status.

This page uses a table in [Softr Databases](/softr-databases/softr-databases-overview). Connecting the data source, inline filters, and search work like they do in the [List](/building-blocks/vikC2AWEpQGkZd4jGyoVxo/list-block/pQhPYuA28VXr5tLLFBcbCU) block. So this page covers the settings that are specific to the Kanban block.

<Tip>You can let users **Drag and Drop** items, [Add Records](/actions/ntEfT9zSJk6aY54G6W5KGU/add-record/3LrUMpQY4BKfDDQj6eb8h8), [Edit Records](/actions/ntEfT9zSJk6aY54G6W5KGU/edit-record/irSJXPF1gCMXNmfsQFscvx), and more in the **Actions** tab of block settings through [Action Buttons](/actions/ntEfT9zSJk6aY54G6W5KGU/action-buttons-overview/idg8Fqf2kjFwcx6kyP3jNq).</Tip>

<Tip>
**Build with AI:** Instead of configuring manually, you can add a Kanban block instantly with the **AI Co-Builder**. Just click the AI icon in the editor and type: *"Create a Kanban board of my tasks grouped by their priority."*
</Tip>

## Prepare your data

To show how the block works, we'll use a **Tasks** table in a Softr Databases database. It has five fields:

- **Task** (Short Text)
- **Details** (Long Text)
- **Status** (Select) with the options To do, In progress, In review, and Done
- **Priority** (Select) with the options High, Medium, and Low
- **Assignee email** (Email)

<Frame caption="The Tasks table in Softr Databases.">![The Tasks table in Softr Databases, with the fields Task, Details, Status, Priority, and Assignee email](/building-blocks/images/kanban/tasks-table.webp)</Frame>

Learn more about the available [field types](/softr-databases/field-types).

## Connect the block to your table

1. Add a Kanban block to your page.
2. Open the **Source** tab of the block settings.
3. Select **Softr Databases** as the account.
4. Select the database, then select the table.

<Frame caption="The Source tab: the account, the database, and the table.">![The Source tab of the Kanban block. Numbers point to the Source tab, the Softr Databases account, the database, and the table](/building-blocks/images/kanban/source-tab-annotated.webp)</Frame>

## Group by

Open the **Content** tab. Under **Group by**, select the field that sets the columns. In our example, we select **Status**.

The board gets one column for each option of the field: To do, In progress, In review, and Done. Each card sits in the column that matches its value.

<Frame caption="The Content tab: the title of the board, Group by, and the columns.">![The Content tab of the Kanban block. Numbers point to the title, the Group by field, and the columns](/building-blocks/images/kanban/group-by-annotated.webp)</Frame>

By default, **Show all available options** is on, so every option of the field becomes a column. Turn it off to choose the columns yourself.

## Choose what shows on each card

Under **Item fields**, map the fields that appear on the cards. In our example, **Task** is the heading, **Details** is the text, and **Priority** is a tag.

The footer shows two short fields side by side, such as a tag or a date.

Under **Column options**, choose the style of the board (Standard, Minimal, or Joined), the column width, and the height. You can also show the card count and hide overflowing columns.

<Frame caption="Item fields and column options.">![The Content tab of the Kanban block, further down. Numbers point to the item fields, the footer fields, and the column options](/building-blocks/images/kanban/card-fields-annotated.webp)</Frame>

## Show each user only their tasks

Kanban's conditional filters work like those of the [List](/building-blocks/vikC2AWEpQGkZd4jGyoVxo/list-block/pQhPYuA28VXr5tLLFBcbCU) block. They are in the **Source** tab, under **Record filters**.

For our example, each logged-in user should see only their own tasks. To do that, add a condition: **Assignee email** is the logged-in user's email.

1. Under **Record filters**, click **Add condition**.
2. Select the **Assignee email** field.
3. Select **Is**.
4. Select the logged-in user's email as the value.

<Frame caption="A record filter: Assignee email is the logged-in user's email.">![The Record filters section of the Kanban block. Numbers point to the match setting and to the condition on the Assignee email field](/building-blocks/images/kanban/record-filter-annotated.webp)</Frame>

Here is what **maria@acme.com** sees after logging in and opening the board.

<Frame caption="The board of one logged-in user.">![A Kanban board that shows only the tasks assigned to one user](/building-blocks/images/kanban/board-one-user.webp)</Frame>

## Move cards between columns

If the **Drag and drop** toggle is on under **Actions**, logged-in users can drag cards between columns. You can also let only specific user groups move cards, through the [visibility settings](/user-groups-and-permissions/3oA6AaFzUiePs2sW2TpYrF/action-button-visibility/tqpb8TkUD4U4DCAUA2hjKo) of the toggle.

<Frame caption="Dragging a card from In progress to In review.">![A card in the middle of a drag from the In progress column to the In review column](/building-blocks/images/kanban/drag-card.webp)</Frame>

When a user moves a card to another column, the value of the card changes in the table too. In our example, we move **Design onboarding checklist** from **In progress** to **In review**. In Softr Databases, its **Status** changes to **In review**.

<Frame caption="Before the move, the Status of the task is In progress.">![The Tasks table before the move. The Status of Design onboarding checklist is In progress](/building-blocks/images/kanban/status-before.webp)</Frame>

<Frame caption="After the move, the Status of the task is In review.">![The Tasks table after the move. The Status of Design onboarding checklist is In review](/building-blocks/images/kanban/status-after.webp)</Frame>
