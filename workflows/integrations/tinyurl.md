# TinyURL integration

Connect TinyURL with your Softr applications to turn long, unwieldy URLs into short, shareable links automatically. Build workflows that shorten links the moment they're needed — when a form is submitted, a record is added, or a new member signs up — so every link you share is clean and easy to pass along.

## Overview

The Softr TinyURL integration links your no-code apps directly to your TinyURL account. Whenever your workflow produces a URL worth sharing — a referral link, a campaign page, a personalized signup link — you can automatically shorten it into a TinyURL link with a custom alias, your choice of short domain, and optional tags and expiration, then store or send the short link anywhere in your app.

Whether you're building a marketing dashboard, a member portal, or a referral program, the TinyURL integration keeps every shared link short, readable, and on-message — powered by one of the longest-running URL shorteners on the web.

## Available Actions

### Create link

Shorten any URL into a TinyURL link with an optional custom alias, short domain (tinyurl.com, tiny.one, or rotf.lol), tags, expiration date, and description — all set dynamically from your Softr workflow. The short link comes back ready to store in a record, send in an email, or show to your users.

## Key Benefits

- **No-code simplicity:** Configure TinyURL actions visually in Softr — no code required beyond the one-time API token setup.
- **Short links on autopilot:** Shorten URLs the moment your workflow runs, instead of pasting them into TinyURL by hand.
- **Readable, memorable links:** Give each link a custom alias like `tinyurl.com/spring-sale` instead of a random code.
- **Links that expire when you want:** Set an expiration date on time-limited links — promos, invitations, early-access offers — so they stop working on schedule.
- **Share-ready output:** Use the short link immediately in the next workflow step — save it to a record, send it via email or Slack, or display it in your app.

## How to Connect Softr with TinyURL

1. Open your Softr workspace and go to **Workflows**.
2. Create a new workflow or open an existing one, then add a TinyURL action.
3. In the **Account** field, click **Add another account** and paste your TinyURL **API token**.
4. To create an API token, open TinyURL and go to **Account → Settings → API**, then generate a new token.
5. Click **Save** — Softr verifies your token and connects the account.
6. Map the **Destination URL** from your Softr forms, records, or previous workflow steps, optionally pick a domain and add an alias, tags, or an expiration date, then activate your workflow.
