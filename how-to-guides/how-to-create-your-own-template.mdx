You can turn any app you've built in Softr into a **template** that other people can clone into their own workspace, complete with its pages, blocks, styling, connected database, and workflows. Share it with a link or submit it to be featured on [**Softr Universe**](/affiliate-and-referral-programs/softr-universe).

<Note>
  Creating a template works for apps built on **Softr Databases** as well as apps connected to **Airtable**. (Earlier, template creation was limited to Airtable-based apps — that's no longer the case.) When someone clones your template, Softr copies the app along with its data source and workflows so they get a working starting point, not an empty shell.
</Note>

## Why create a template

Templates are a fast way to package and reuse work you've already done:

- **Speed up client and agency work** — build an app once and spin up a fresh copy for each new client instead of starting from scratch.
- **Sell your work** — package an app as a template and sell it, for example through a [**Gumroad**](/integrations/gumroad) storefront.
- **Generate leads** — offer a useful template for free to attract potential customers to your product or service.
- **Showcase your no-code expertise** — publish polished templates to build a reputation in the community.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Applications">
    In Softr Studio, go to **Applications** in the left sidebar. Your apps are listed under the **All** tab.

    <Frame caption="The Applications dashboard, listing your apps.">

    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app's actions menu">
    Hover over the app you want to turn into a template and click the **⋯** (more actions) button on its card. The menu includes **Duplicate**, **Share**, **Move**, **Create a template**, **View connections**, **Change thumbnail**, and **Delete**.

    <Frame caption="The ⋯ menu on an app card, with 'Create a template' highlighted.">
      {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: Hover an app card, click the "⋯" button, and capture the open dropdown showing all items with "Create a template" in focus. */}

      ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 56 41 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-at-11.56.41-AM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 56 41 AM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create a template">
    Choose **Create a template**. The **Create a template from this app** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the template details">
    Complete the fields in the dialog:

    - **Template name** — a short, descriptive name.
    - **Description** — a few sentences describing what the template does.
    - **Thumbnail** — click **Upload thumbnail** to add a preview image that represents the template.

    <Frame caption="The 'Create a template from this app' dialog.">
      {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: Capture the full "Create a template from this app" dialog showing the Template name, Description, and Thumbnail (Upload thumbnail) fields, the "Heads up!" notice, and the Cancel / Create template buttons. */}

      ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 57 33 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-at-11.57.33-AM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 57 33 AM")
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      As the dialog's **Heads up!** notice reminds you: make sure you're not sharing personal or sensitive data within your template. Anyone who clones the template receives a copy of the connected data.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the template">
    Click **Create template**. Your new template is saved to your workspace and appears under the **Templates** tab (see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For an app connected to **Airtable**, make sure the underlying Airtable base is set up so that clones get their own editable copy of the data — typically by sharing the base with a public, read-only link that has **"Allow viewers to copy data out of this base"** enabled. See [**Airtable**](/data-sources/airtable) for how Softr connects to Airtable.

## Find and manage your templates

Templates you create live under **Applications → Templates**. If you haven't made any yet, you'll see _"You have not created any custom templates."_ with a link to learn how.

## Share your template

Your template isn't visible to anyone else automatically. You can distribute it in two ways.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Share a direct link">
    Share your template's link directly with the people you want to be able to clone it. Anyone who opens the link can duplicate the template into their own Softr workspace.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 59 32 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-at-11.59.32-AM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 59 32 AM")
    </Frame>

    {/* VERIFY: The exact "get a shareable link" entry point (and whether the legacy `?show-toolbar=true` URL parameter on a published app is still the mechanism) was not re-verified live in this pass. Confirm the current sharing flow in Studio and update this section with the precise clicks before publishing. */}
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Submit to Softr Universe">
    Submit your template to [**Softr Universe**](/affiliate-and-referral-programs/softr-universe) to have it featured in Softr's public gallery, where the wider community can discover and clone it.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 59 32 AM 1](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-10-at-11.59.32-AM-1.png "Screenshot 2026 08 10 At 11 59 32 AM 1")
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  When someone clones your template, permissions configured on individual blocks (such as visibility or edit permissions) may be reset in their copy, and data-source–specific configuration is preserved only when their workspace connects to a compatible data source. Have them review the app's connections after cloning.
</Note>
