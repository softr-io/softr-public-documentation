Welcome to the Softr Help Docs. Here you'll find what you need to build business apps with Softr, from your first prompt in the AI Co-Builder to the permissions of each user group. We add and update articles as the product grows.

If you can't find an answer, ask the [Softr Community](https://community.softr.io/). You can also contact our support team through the live chat on any page.

## Where to start

- **New to Softr?** Read [App Building 101](/core-concepts-overview/pages-and-navigation). It walks through each step of building an app, from pages to publishing.
- **Want a head start?** [Generate an app with the AI Co-Builder](/start-here/ai-co-builder), or [create an app from a template](/start-here/create-an-app-from-a-template).
- **Need a hand?** [Hire a Softr expert](https://www.softr.io/partners) to build your app with you or review it.

## How the docs are organized

The tabs at the top of each page split the docs by part of Softr. Each how-to guide sits next to the feature it uses.

- **Get Started**: the basics, a step-by-step overview of how a Softr app comes together, use cases, and your account and billing.
- **Apps**: blocks, actions, styling, app settings, publishing, and app integrations.
- **Data**: Softr Databases, the external data sources you can connect, and how to fix data source errors.
- **Users & Security**: sign-in, user groups, permissions, and compliance.
- **Workflows & Agents**: Softr Workflows and the AI agents that work on your data.
- **Developers**: the Softr API, the MCP servers, and custom code.

To find a specific topic, use the search bar at the top of the page. Many articles end with an FAQ section that answers common questions on that topic.
